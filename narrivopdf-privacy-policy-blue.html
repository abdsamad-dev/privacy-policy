Privacy Policy

NarrivoPDF is a free PDF tools platform designed for simple, privacy-conscious document work without requiring an account. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use our tools and optional online or AI features.

1. Who we are

NarrivoPDF (“NarrivoPDF,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a PDF tools platform available through its website and Android application.

Website: https://narrivopdf.in/

NarrivoPDF is designed to let users access its PDF tools without creating an account. This policy applies to the NarrivoPDF app and website that link to this page.

2. Accounts and payment

NarrivoPDF does not require login or signup to use its tools. The app is intended to be free to use, and this policy does not describe an account subscription system.

We do not ask users to create a profile or provide an email address just to use the app’s PDF tools. If a future version adds accounts, payments, subscriptions, or other features that change the information handled, this policy and the app’s disclosures must be updated before those features are released.

3. Information handled

The information handled depends on which tool you use and whether it needs an internet connection.

  • Files and document content: the files you select and the content needed to perform the operation you request.
  • AI prompts and selected text: if you use an AI feature, the prompt and any document text or context supplied to that feature may be processed to generate an answer.
  • Technical and security data: online services and hosting infrastructure may process information such as IP address, request time, error details, and security logs, depending on their configuration.
  • AI usage metadata: where AI requests go through a backend, the service may record usage information such as prompt/completion token counts, request status, timestamps, model metadata, or request identifiers. This is technical usage logging, not an account signup system.

We do not intentionally request unrelated personal information to perform a local PDF operation. The final app and server configuration should be checked for any analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs before making broader claims about data collection.

4. PDF files and local processing

NarrivoPDF is designed with an offline-first approach. When a tool runs entirely on your device, its file processing happens locally and that tool does not upload the document to our server.

Some features may need an internet connection, including any feature that calls an online backend or external AI, OCR, translation, or conversion provider. For those features, the content necessary to fulfill your request may leave your device and be processed by the relevant service.

For sensitive documents: Prefer tools that operate fully offline. Do not submit confidential content to an online feature unless you understand and accept the associated processing.

File access is limited to the files you choose or authorize through Android’s file access mechanisms, subject to the behavior of the installed version.

5. AI features

If AI features are available in your version of NarrivoPDF, such as summarization, document chat, translation, question generation, or writing assistance, the relevant prompt and selected document text may be processed on-device or sent to an online service, depending on the feature and its configuration.

  • On-device AI: inference is performed locally for that feature; separate network activity by other features may still occur.
  • Online AI: the prompt, selected text, and context needed to answer may be transmitted to the configured backend or AI provider.
  • Usage logs: an online backend may record token counts and request metadata for usage tracking, reliability, security, or limits. Whether full prompt or response text is stored depends on the actual deployed logging configuration.
  • AI accuracy: generated content may be incomplete or incorrect. Review AI output before relying on it.

Online AI providers, if enabled, process data under their own terms and privacy policies. The specific providers and whether prompt/response content is retained must match the actual release configuration. Do not enable or list a provider in the product disclosures unless it is actually used.

6. How information is used

Information may be processed only as needed to:

  • perform the PDF operation you request;
  • generate an AI response when you choose an AI feature;
  • operate online endpoints and maintain service reliability;
  • measure AI usage and enforce technical limits where applicable;
  • detect abuse, investigate errors, and protect service security; and
  • respond to a request you send to us or comply with applicable law.

Do not use document content or AI prompts for advertising or unrelated purposes unless the policy is updated and any required notice or consent is provided.

7. Sharing and service providers

Local PDF tools do not need to share document content with an external provider when they run fully on-device. For features that rely on online infrastructure, information may be processed by the hosting provider and any AI, OCR, translation, or conversion provider used by that feature.

We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the safety, rights, or security of users and the service.

The exact third-party services, SDKs, and data recipients depend on the deployed website/app configuration. These must be reviewed before the Google Play Data Safety form is submitted.

8. Retention and deletion

Files processed locally generally remain in the locations you choose, unless you save, export, or delete them. Temporary files created by the app should be cleaned up according to the behavior implemented in the released version.

Online request and security logs may be retained for the period needed for service operation, security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance. The exact retention period depends on the backend and hosting configuration and should be defined and verified before release.

Because no account is required, there is no standard app account to delete. To ask about online logs or other information associated with a request, use the contact route on the official website. Any deletion request is subject to applicable law, technical feasibility, and legitimate security or legal retention requirements.

9. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the app and its online services. Production network endpoints should use HTTPS, restrict administrative access, and protect server credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Never share private credentials or authentication tokens. Do not store privileged backend keys in the Android application package or a public source repository.

10. Children’s privacy

NarrivoPDF is a general-purpose PDF utility. It is not intended to knowingly collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, contact us through the official website so the issue can be reviewed.

11. Your choices and rights

You choose which files to open and which features to use. You can avoid online or AI features if you do not want the related content to be transmitted for processing, though those features may then be unavailable. Local file management and deletion are controlled by you and the relevant Android storage location.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by online services. Contact us through the official website to make a request.

12. International processing

If an online feature or hosting provider processes information in another country, the information may be transferred across borders. The actual hosting regions and providers depend on the deployment configuration.

13. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app, its online services, or applicable legal requirements change. We will revise the “Last updated” date when we publish a new version. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
